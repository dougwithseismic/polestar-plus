Use Battle Shout when you do not have it.
Use Bloodrage when you have a target, rage is under 20 and your health is 50% or more (as far as the game shows).
Use Attack when you have a target. It is switched on once and left on.
Use Bloodrage when you are fighting something, rage is under 20 and your health is 50% or more (as far as the game shows).
Use Thunder Clap when you are fighting something, 2 or more enemies are in reach and it is not on the target.
Use Execute when you have a target and the target's health is under 20% (as far as the game shows).
Use Overpower when you have a target. It answers a dodge, block or parry: it comes up when the game lights it.
Use Revenge when you have a target. It answers a dodge, block or parry: it comes up when the game lights it.
Use Sunder Armor when you are fighting something and either it is stacked fewer than 5 times or it has under 4.5 seconds left.
Use Thunder Clap when you are fighting something, the target is elite and it is not on the target.
Use Rend when you are fighting something, it is not on the target and the target's health is 35% or more (as far as the game shows).
Use Sunder Armor when you are fighting something, the target is elite and rage is 30 or more.
Use Heroic Strike when you are fighting something and rage is 30 or more. It is switched on once and left on.
